## [JoyAI-Video-Edit: Real-Time 720p Video Editing at 30 FPS](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.03974) ⭐️ 8.0/10

JoyAI-Video-Edit introduces a 16B-parameter autoregressive diffusion framework for real-time, open-ended video editing, achieving 720p at approximately 30 FPS on a single Nvidia B200 GPU. It combines chunk-wise autoregressive adaptation, Source-Anchored Distribution Matching Distillation (SA-DMD), and Long-Horizon Autoregressive Distillation to address temporal consistency and source fidelity. This work significantly advances real-time video editing by enabling high-resolution (720p) editing at interactive frame rates on a single GPU, which was previously challenging. It outperforms existing streaming editors and remains competitive with offline systems, potentially impacting video production, content creation, and interactive applications. The framework uses a chunk-wise autoregressive approach to avoid access to future frames and predefined duration, and employs SA-DMD to preserve source fidelity during two-step generation. Long-Horizon Autoregressive Distillation mitigates accumulated temporal drift, and the code is available on GitHub.

huggingface_papers · Hugging Face Papers · Aug 5, 00:00

**Background**: Autoregressive diffusion models (ARDMs) combine autoregressive factorization with diffusion-based denoising, enabling efficient generation of sequences. Distribution Matching Distillation (DMD) is a technique to distill a multi-step diffusion model into fewer steps while preserving distribution fidelity. Long-horizon autoregressive distillation addresses drift in long-sequence generation by using teacher rollouts for supervision.

## [AURORA-LM: Continuous-Latent Diffusion Language Model with High-Capacity Decodable Latents](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.02602) ⭐️ 8.0/10

AURORA-LM introduces a continuous-latent diffusion language model that separates decodable text representation from distribution modeling, using a Query-based Encoder-Decoder and a Block-causal Diffusion Transformer. It achieves the strongest performance among evaluated continuous and diffusion-based language models on OpenWebText free generation and XSum summarization, scaling to 1B parameters. This work addresses a key limitation in continuous-latent diffusion language models by preserving high-capacity decodable latents, potentially improving text generation fidelity and efficiency. It could influence future research in generative models for text and bridge the gap between continuous latent modeling in other modalities and language. AURORA-LM restricts only the noisy-input pathway while retaining the full clean-latent prediction target, accommodating full-width latents without reducing decoder-facing capacity. It also calibrates the noise-level distribution to latent width and introduces self-trajectory consistency to bridge training noise and inference denoising. Experiments were conducted on Ascend NPUs.

huggingface_papers · Hugging Face Papers · Aug 5, 00:00

**Background**: Continuous-latent diffusion models have been successful for images, video, and audio, but text generation still relies on discrete tokens. Existing continuous language models either inherit embedding spaces not designed for joint generation and decoding, or compress autoencoded latents, sacrificing fidelity. AURORA-LM aims to directly model the distribution of high-capacity decodable text latents using diffusion, avoiding these compromises.

The IETF Braid draft proposes a standard for HTTP subscriptions to enable real-time synchronization over persistent connections.
